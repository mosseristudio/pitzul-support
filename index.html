Support – Pitzul

Pitzul shares out the expenses of a trip or an event: one person enters them from their phone, and
at the end everyone knows who pays whom, and how much.

Contact

A question, a problem or an idea: write to mosseri.studio@gmail.com. We read every message and answer in Hebrew or English.

Questions people ask

Where does the odd cent go? Every amount is kept in whole cents. When an amount doesn't divide
exactly, everyone first gets their share rounded down, and the cents left over go out one at a
time: first to whoever's division left the largest remainder, and in a tie by the order people
were added to the trip. 100.00 between three is 33.34, 33.33 and 33.33, so the shares always add
up to exactly the expense.

Why isn't the list of transfers always the shortest? It's built from the largest debt to the
largest credit. That's simple, predictable and usually short, but sometimes one transfer fewer is
possible. Always finding the shortest is a very hard problem to compute.

How do I move a trip to another phone? In the trip's menu: "Export a backup". On the other
phone: "Import a backup" on the trips screen. Importing always adds a new trip and never
overwrites anything.

Why can't I delete a person? Because they still have expenses. The screen shows which can be
fixed automatically and which you need to open and fix. Once they have none left, deleting works.

I deleted an expense by mistake. Right after deleting there's "Undo". Later: the trip's menu →
"Restore the deleted expense" (the last one deleted).

Can I pay through the app? No. Each transfer has "Copy amount", to paste into Bit, PayBox or
anything else.
