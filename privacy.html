Privacy Policy – Pitzul

Last updated: October 2026

Pitzul is an app for sharing expenses on a trip or at an event: one person enters the expenses,
and the app works out what each person paid, what they owe, and who pays whom. It is built so
that we know nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The app doesn't collect, send or store any data with us: no account or sign-up, no
server, no ads, no analytics or usage measurement, no crash reporting, and no tracking. The app
doesn't connect to the internet at all.

What is stored, and where

On the device only, in the app's private folder: your trips (name, dates, currency), the
participants' names and each one's emoji or photo, the expenses (name, amount, who paid, who took
part, category and date), the last deleted expense (so you can bring it back), and your display
and language preferences.

A participant's photo is kept resized and without its embedded details: no location, no
device model and no date taken. Temporary copies made on the way (when picking a photo or sharing
a file) are deleted.

If your phone's backup is on (iCloud or Google), this data is usually included in it, along with
the rest of the phone. That backup is yours and lives in your account, not with us.

We have no way to see this data, restore it or delete it for you, because it never reaches us.

Information about other people

Participants' names and photos usually belong to your friends, not to you. They are stored only
on your phone. Before adding someone's photo, or sending a summary that includes their name,
make sure they're fine with it.

Permissions

Camera – only when you tap "Take a photo", to take a picture for a participant. That is its
only use. The photo is resized, its embedded details are removed, and it is kept on the phone
only, not in your photo library. On Android the app opens the phone's own camera app, so it asks
for no camera permission at all. You can say no and the app keeps working: you can pick a
photo from your library, or an emoji.

Photos – picking a photo uses the phone's own photo picker, which hands the app only the photo
you chose. That's why the app never asks for access to your whole library.

Sharing

When you share a personal summary (an image), a general summary (a PDF) or a trip backup, the
file is created on the device and handed to the phone's share sheet. It contains the trip's
names, amounts and expenses; a personal summary also shows that participant's photo, and a backup
contains everyone's photos (without their embedded details). A deleted expense is not included in
a backup. Where it goes from there is your choice; we are not involved.

"Copy amount" puts a number on the phone's clipboard, nothing more. The app makes no payments and
doesn't connect to payment apps.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Deleting data

Deleting a trip in the app deletes it, its expenses and its participants' photos from the phone.
Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data. Files you shared or exported earlier stay wherever
you sent them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with its update date.
