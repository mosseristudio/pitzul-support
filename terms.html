Last updated: October 2026

What the app does

Pitzul helps share the expenses of a trip or an event: you enter who paid and who took part in
each expense, and it works out each person's share, their balance, and a list of transfers that
settles the account. It is free, with no ads and no purchases.

The calculation

Every amount is calculated in whole cents (agorot), and when an amount doesn't divide exactly,
the cents left over are given out by a fixed rule (described on the support page). The list of
transfers keeps their number low, but doesn't always reach the smallest possible number. The app
makes no payments: the transfers themselves happen outside it, under your responsibility.

What is out of our control

The data is stored only on your phone. If the phone is lost or reset, or the app is deleted with
no backup, we have no way to restore it. Exporting a trip backup is how you keep a copy.
